Logos identify Simon's career and education history; original proportions are preserved.

Dark-background treatment:
- AWS: original SVG path geometry, white wordmark and Amazon Orange (#FF9900) smile, matching the two-color dark-background treatment documented at https://www.amazonaws.cn/en/trademark-guidelines/.
- Snowflake: original white transparent PNG from the official logo archive.
- Personio: original vector paths rendered in white.
- Arctis and fortiss: original alpha-transparent assets rendered as white silhouettes with CSS, preserving geometry.
- TUM and KTH: original white SVG marks.
- No logo container has a background or border.
- CSEE: original SVG embedded in the official https://csee.tech/ website bundle at https://csee.tech/assets/index-BJq0aBWJ.js; displayed in its original white/blue on transparent.

- TNG: official transparent SVG from https://www.tngtech.com/_astro/TNG_Logo.URm66zYr_2cdO3R.svg ; displayed in white with CSS on the dark background.
